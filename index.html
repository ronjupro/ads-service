Submit a photo campaign in the Telegram bot. An admin approves it; the bot attempts to post to verified publisher channels.

How do I verify a channel?

You must be a channel admin. Add the bot as an administrator with post permission, then send /channel @yourchannel.

Are withdrawals live?

No. This starter has no real payment integration.
